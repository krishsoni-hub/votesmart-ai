What is Voting?

Voting is a democratic right where citizens elect leaders to represent them in the government.

Minimum Age

You must be at least 18 years old on the qualifying date (usually Jan 1st of the election year) to vote in India.

Voter ID (EPIC)

EPIC stands for Electors Photo Identity Card. It's your official proof of registration as a voter.

Election Process

Check name on roll → Bring Voter ID to booth → Verify ID → Cast vote on EVM → Verify on VVPAT.
